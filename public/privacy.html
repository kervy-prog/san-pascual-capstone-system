SAN PASCUAL CONNECT

Privacy Notice

Effective October 2026

Who operates this service

San Pascual Connect is the official infrastructure report management system of Barangay San Pascual, San Narciso, Zambales, Philippines. It is operated by the barangay administration to receive and manage infrastructure reports from residents.

What information we collect

To provide the service, we collect the details you enter when registering and submitting reports: name, contact details, address, account credentials, report descriptions, uploaded photos, and verification documents required to confirm residency or official designation.

Why we collect it

Your information is used only to verify your account, process and respond to your infrastructure reports, and allow authorized barangay officials to act on them. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for marketing.

Who can access it

Only authorized barangay administrators and officials can access submitted information, and only for the purposes described above.

How long we keep it

Records are retained for as long as needed to administer barangay services and comply with applicable legal obligations, after which they are removed.

Your rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting the Barangay San Pascual office during regular office hours.

Contact

Barangay San Pascual Office, San Narciso, Zambales, Philippines.
